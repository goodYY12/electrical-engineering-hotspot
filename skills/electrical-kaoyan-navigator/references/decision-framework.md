# Decision framework

Keep independent dimensions rather than collapsing them into a pseudo-scientific total:

1. supply: actual unified quota, recommendation pressure, expansion/contraction, special share, small sample
2. competition: applicant/first-choice/retest/admission counts, retest ratio, transfer policy
3. scores: retest line versus admitted distribution, volatility, structural breaks
4. subject: scope, evidence-based difficulty, stability, change signals, resources, migration
5. retest: weight, written/interview/English/content, differential ratio, single-item elimination, observed reversals
6. popularity: organic versus commercial weak signals only
7. structure: college/program/project reorganization and code/direction changes
8. uncertainty: gaps, conflicts, missing archives, reliance on social evidence

Position a target as `冲`, `偏冲`, `稳中偏冲`, `稳`, `稳中偏保`, or `保`, always with confidence and reasons. Low risk with low confidence is not safe.

Use the user's background, current subjects/progress, estimated score interval, time, degree/study-mode preferences, transfer/joint-training acceptance, region and career goals. Missing profile fields lower confidence but do not halt analysis.

Target-score bands are historical preparation targets, never future line predictions. Lower confidence after major subject changes, quota structure changes, or other structural breaks.

Trigger reassessment after catalogue, subject, syllabus, recommendation result, quota, registration close, or retest-rule events; flag major subject change, clear contraction, recommendation surge, or college reorganization.
