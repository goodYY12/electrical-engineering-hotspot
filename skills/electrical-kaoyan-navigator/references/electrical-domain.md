# Electrical engineering domain model

## Programs and directions

Prioritize `080800 电气工程` (academic) and `085801 电气工程` (professional). Treat a same-code program in another college, excellent-engineer school, joint base, campus, study mode, or special program as a separate admissions entity unless an official document proves a shared admissions scope.

Map official direction names to, but never replace them with, canonical themes:

- power systems and automation
- electrical machines and apparatus
- high voltage and insulation
- power electronics and drives
- electrical theory and new technology
- renewable energy, storage, smart grid, integrated energy, electricity markets
- flexible DC, advanced machines, power semiconductors, intelligent sensing, AI plus power systems

Separate research strength from admission difficulty. Identify whether admission is unified at discipline level or direction-specific and whether direction affects retest or supervisor selection.

## Subject profile

Normalize subject topics including circuits, power-system steady/transient analysis, machines, power electronics, automatic control, electromagnetic fields, analog/digital electronics, relay protection, high voltage, and electrical engineering comprehensive.

For each cycle retain official code/name, total marks, syllabus URL/hash, topics, textbooks (author/publisher/edition/chapters), question types, and retest subject.

## Change detection

Compare content before labels. Classify:

- A: code only
- B: name changed, content substantially equivalent
- C: textbook edition only
- D: partial syllabus adjustment
- E: principal content changed
- F: complete replacement

Report added/removed topics, effective admission year, evidence, confidence, and migration cost. A code change alone is not a major change.

## Migration cost

Estimate `low`, `medium`, `high`, or `unknown` from topic overlap, new textbooks/chapters, past-paper familiarity, and time remaining. Report the missing topics; do not emit a fake precise score.

## Cross-school scores and “score suppression”

Never infer that one school's self-set subject is easier merely from higher raw scores. Difficulty evidence may use within-school cross-year distributions, syllabus breadth, official papers/samples, question structure, calculation load, changes, and agreement among independent experiences.

Treat claims of strict scoring as unverified unless multi-year, sufficiently large, verifiable score samples show an abnormal deviation not explained by exam difficulty. Use the wording: community feedback exists, but official or adequate sample evidence is insufficient.

## Employment

Prefer official employment reports, college reports, public employer recruitment/admission records, and official cooperation/news. Model employer sectors separately. “Grid recognition” from community posts is a community claim, not an official ranking.
