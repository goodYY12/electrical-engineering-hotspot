# Research methodology

Define the target entity and cycle before searching. Acquire the most recent five completed cycles when possible. Archive first, parse second, normalize third; do not hand-copy facts directly into conclusions.

For every cycle:

1. Identify catalogue and exact program scope.
2. Capture plan and recommendation evidence without assuming unified quota.
3. Capture subjects, syllabus, books, and retest rules.
4. Parse retest/admission rosters when public; minimize personal data.
5. Compute cohort-specific statistics.
6. Compare adjacent cycles for subject, quota, score, and structural changes.
7. Detect conflicts and quality issues.
8. Separate official facts, calculations, community observations, judgments, and unknowns.

Manual verification records must name the evidence ID, expected fact, source location, checker, date, and result. Five-school E2E cases should cover a comprehensive 985, an electricity-industry institution, another 211/double-first-class institution, a strong provincial institution, and a school with subject change. Do not hardcode school-specific extraction logic merely to pass a case.
